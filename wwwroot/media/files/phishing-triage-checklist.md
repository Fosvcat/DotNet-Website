# Phishing Triage Checklist

## Pause

- Do not reply, open unexpected attachments or use embedded sign-in links.
- Preserve the original message.

## Verify

- Inspect the full sender and reply-to domains.
- Confirm unusual requests through a previously trusted channel.
- Check the real destination hostname without visiting it.

## Report

- Use the organization's approved phishing-reporting workflow.
- Include what was clicked, opened or entered.

## Contain

- Reset exposed credentials and revoke active sessions.
- Isolate affected devices when policy requires it.
- Retain the message and timeline for investigation.

# CIA Triad Review Checklist

## Asset and ownership

- What information or service are we protecting?
- Who owns it, and who is authorized to use it?
- What classification and retention rules apply?

## Confidentiality

- Is access based on least privilege?
- Is sensitive data encrypted in transit and at rest?
- Are access reviews and authentication controls documented?

## Integrity

- Can unauthorized changes be detected?
- Are releases signed or compared against trusted hashes?
- Are important events written to protected audit logs?

## Availability

- Are recovery time and recovery point objectives defined?
- Are backups tested rather than merely created?
- Are monitoring, capacity and failover procedures exercised?
